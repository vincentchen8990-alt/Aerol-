# Safety, limitations and uncertainty

**This repository is a research/demo artifact. Do not use its outputs to direct a person's medical treatment, set a real spacecraft's artificial-gravity exposure, or command physical motors without independent safety engineering.**

1. **Catalogs are not training data.** The five supplied research files contain metadata, not genuine health measurements from individuals. The project deliberately does not fabricate human records.
2. **Empatica feature limitations.** EmbracePlus does not directly measure DXA bone density or cuff blood pressure. Optical pulse-rate variability does not imply ECG-derived HRV. Wrist accelerometry does not independently give the crew-floor gravity vector.
3. **Earth vs space.** NHANES cross-sectional associations, pediatric sleep cohorts, stress tests, clinical respiratory recordings and ground centrifuge results cannot be directly combined into an astronaut gravity prescription.
4. **Clinical evidence missing.** No validated individual causal relationship exists in these included files linking an artificial-gravity change to future BMD and cardiovascular improvements. The prescription endpoint returns `insufficient_evidence`.
5. **Shared habitat constraint.** Occupants at the same ring radius share a mechanical gravity exposure; arbitrary person-specific levels are not simultaneously available.
6. **Simplified mechanics.** Fixed-axis, rigid-body, ideal axial-thrust Newtonian model, finite but assumed actuator parameters. Excludes full attitude, flexural deformation, spacecraft propulsion power, fuel, vibration/habitat design and human motion sickness.
7. **Data privacy.** Do not publish identifiable participant medical data or circumvent controlled datasets. Dataset access licenses are reviewed source-by-source.
8. **Training metrics.** Synthetic model accuracy is measured against its own generator, not real spacecraft recordings. Model confidence does not certify physical safety.
9. **Frontend.** The builtin 3D projection works offline through FastAPI; the React/Three.js code needs third-party packages installed in an Internet-enabled environment. The archived-ISS mode exists only in the builtin viewer, not in the React source.
10. **Archived ISS measurements.** The MARS-S model forecasts one second of already-controlled rotor RPM from eight measured samples; the logs contain no command, torque, current or inertia, so it is not a torque-response model or controller, its recursive forecasts failed, and some one-step outputs are negative (shown invalid). The derived nominal radial g at 0.15 m is a calculation, not measured gravity or crew exposure. The PIMS model forecasts a filtered quasi-steady accelerometer series at one location, 16 s ahead; its advantage over Ridge is negligible. The two datasets are years apart and must not be combined as simultaneous telemetry. The ISS view is a schematic proportional to four published extents, not CAD geometry; 419,725 kg is a reference configuration mass. None of this validates the hypothetical 100 m ring or a full spacecraft. See [MEASURED_ISS](MEASURED_ISS.md).
