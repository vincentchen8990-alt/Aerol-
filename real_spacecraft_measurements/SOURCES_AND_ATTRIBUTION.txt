2. Hardware configuration and mission history
JAXA/Tohoku Medical Megabank, ibSLS mission overview:
https://ibsls.megabank.tohoku.ac.jp/overview
Both MHU-1 and MHU-2 list MARS-S, 0.15 m nominal radius and 77 RPM nominal rotation setting. Logs used are 2016 and2017; publication/release year is 2026.
JAXA program catalogue updated May 27, 2026:
https://humans-in-space.jaxa.jp/kibouser/subject/tag_10.html
Later mission listings demonstrate a continuing program, not real-time rotation status.
CBEF-L facility description:
https://humans-in-space.jaxa.jp/en/biz-lab/experiment/facility/pm/cbef-l/
CBEF-L is later equipment; its generic reference radii must not replace the specific MHU-1/2 cage configuration.
